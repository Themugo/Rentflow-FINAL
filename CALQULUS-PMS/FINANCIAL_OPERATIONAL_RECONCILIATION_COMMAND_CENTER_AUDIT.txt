CALQULUS PMS — Financial & Operational Reconciliation Command Center

Scope
- Canonical reconciliation_cases model across lease→invoice, invoice→payment,
  payment→allocation, bank matching, payout settlement, financial close and evidence.
- Reuses operation_work_items as the canonical ownership/SLA execution layer.
- Manager/submanager scope enforced through can_manage_property_scope.
- No predictive values or fabricated financial metrics introduced.

Controls
- Active-source uniqueness prevents duplicate active cases.
- Reconciliation scan is idempotent for active exceptions.
- Case resolution atomically updates the linked operational work item.
- Evidence may be attached to a case without creating a second document system.
- Critical mismatches are surfaced as critical/high operational work.

Verification
- Static migration/component assertions covered by src/test/financialOperationalReconciliation.test.ts.
- Full application tests depend on installed project dependencies.
