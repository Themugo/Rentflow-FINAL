CALQULUS PMS — Financial Close & Owner Statement Integrity

Implemented:
- Auditable financial_close_periods with immutable close snapshots.
- financial_close_audit records close/reopen actions.
- Manager-scoped period close readiness checks.
- Checks for unmatched bank transactions, pending payments and pending owner payouts.
- Atomic close RPC refuses to lock an unresolved period.
- Reopen RPC requires an explicit reason and records an audit event.
- Dashboard Financial Close Control Center consumes authoritative RPCs only.

Truthfulness:
- No predictive probability is introduced.
- No hard-coded financial values are introduced.
- Period totals derive from invoices, completed payment allocations, expenditures and payout/bank controls.

Verification status:
- Static source assertions: expected to PASS.
- TypeScript/Vitest: run when dependencies are installed; do not infer success when node_modules is absent.
- Remote migration deployment: still requires controlled promotion through the production deployment/reconciliation gate.
